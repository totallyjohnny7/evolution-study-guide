Big Picture · what evolution actually IS
Selection picks people · Evolution moves the population
Diagram · the evolution loop
"Mutation Makes, Selection Sorts, Drift Drifts, Flow Flows."
Background · what counts as evolution

Selection acts on individuals (one organism survives or breeds). Evolution happens to the population (allele frequencies shift between generations). Selection without heritability ≠ evolution.

⭐"V-H-D-F: Variation, Heritable, Differential, Fitness"Darwin's 4 NS requirements. Miss any → no evolution.
⭐"Selection picks PEOPLE; Evolution moves the POPULATION"
Theory ≠ guess. Theory of Evolution sits beside Theory of Gravity — a tested-to-death framework.
— Dobzhansky 1973: "Nothing in biology makes sense except in the light of evolution."

Hardy-Weinberg · the null model
5 conditions hold → frequencies stay flat · break ANY → evolution
Diagram · population-level Punnett
2pq counts BOTH heterozygote squares — that's where the "2" comes from.
Equations · memorize cold
p + q = 1
p² + 2pq + q² = 1
2pq = HETEROZYGOTES
⚠ Trap"2pq = homozygote" → WRONG. 2pq is the HETEROZYGOTE frequency.
⭐"MMSRI"Mutation off · Migration off · Selection off · Random mating · Infinite N. Break ANY → evolution.
Math drill · steps not numbers
  1. Disease frequency = q²
  2. √(q²) = q
  3. p = 1 − q
  4. Carrier frequency = 2pq
🔬 WorkedDisease at 1/10,000 → q² = 0.0001 → q = 0.01 → p ≈ 0.99 → 2pq ≈ 0.02 = 1 in 50 are carriers.
HWE deviation → diagnose mechanism
PatternMechanism

Heterozygote EXCESSHeterozygote advantage (sickle)

Heritability & Breeder's Equation
Big H is whole-genetic · small h is additive-only · only h² predicts response
Diagram · phenotypic variance decomposition
Only h² (narrow / additive) predicts response to selection. Big H does NOT.
Diagram · breeder's equation R = h²S
Realized h² = R/S. Calculated AFTER an experiment by inverting the equation.
Equations · memorize
VP = VG + VE + VG×E
VG = VA + VD + VI
H² = VG/VP   h² = VA/VP
R = h² × S   ·   h² ∈ [0,1]
⚠ Trap"Big H predicts response" → WRONG. Only narrow-sense h² (additive) translates to offspring. Dominance & epistasis don't transmit cleanly.
06
Three Modes of Selection + Frequency-Dependent
Stabilizing squeezes · Directional drives · Disruptive divides
Diagram · the 3 classic modes
"Stabilizing Squeezes · Directional Drives · Disruptive Divides."
Frequency-dependent selection

Negative-FD: rare wins → cycles, maintains polymorphism. Examples: side-blotched lizards (orange/blue/yellow rock-paper-scissors), MHC, prey switching.

Positive-FD: common wins → locks in. Examples: warning coloration, Müllerian mimicry.

⭐"HOT-FV = Balancing"Heterozygote advantage · Negative Frequency-dependence · spatial/temporal Variation. Three sub-types of balancing selection.
Truncation · the breeder's-equation default
Artificial selection: pick a cutoff → every individual above the cutoff breeds. Default for breeder's-equation problems unless told otherwise.
🔬 Sickle-cell trifectaHeterozygote Advantage = Overdominance = Balanced Polymorphism. All synonymous. Robbins switches between them.

Empirical Cases · evolution caught in the act
Fast, real, observed
🔬 Galápagos Finches (Grants)"Dry Drives Deep beaks; Wet welcomes Wee." 1977 drought killed shallow-beaked birds → mean beak depth shifted within ONE generation. Directional selection caught live.
🔬 Peppered Moths (Kettlewell)"Soot Selects Black, Clean returns to Pepper." Industrial melanism — dark form rose with soot, fell when air cleaned.
🔬 Belyaev Foxes"Tame in 50 Generations brings Floppy ears, Spots, Curly tails." Selecting only on tameness pulled along piebald coat, floppy ears, curly tails — pleiotropy of tameness.
🔬 Endler Guppies"Pretty Pools = Pretty Boys; Predator Pools = Plain Boys." Color evolves up/down in 4–14 generations depending on predator presence.

Sex & Sexual Selection
Anisogamy starts everything · OSR drives ornament size
Why sex pays
⭐"Red Queen + Muller's Ratchet"Sex outruns parasites + recombination purges deleterious mutations that asexual lineages accumulate.
Anisogamy — eggs expensive, sperm cheap → all sex-role asymmetries flow from this.
Bateman's principle — females limited by EGGS; males limited by MATES.
Two arenas of sexual selection
⭐"Intra = Inside same sex (combat) · Inter = Between sexes (choice)"
Intrasexual → antlers, horns, body size (males vs males).
Intersexual → peacock tail, bird song, bowerbird art (female choice).
⭐"Skewed OSR = Strong Selection"Operational Sex Ratio: more receptive males than females → fierce competition → bigger ornaments.
Three hypotheses for female choice (not exclusive)
FISHER RUNAWAYTrait + preference become genetically correlated → both escalate together.
ZAHAVI HANDICAPCostly trait HONEST signal — only good genes can afford it.
HAMILTON-ZUK PARASITESBright = parasite-resistant → choosing him gives offspring resistant genes.
Post-mating: sperm compete; females cryptic-choose (sperm storage, cervical filter, etc.).

Species & Speciation
4 concepts · pre/post-zygotic · ASP modes · Haldane's rule
4 species concepts
⭐"BMPE: Best Mammals Play Evens"
  1. Biological (Mayr) — interbreed + reproductively isolated
  2. Morphological — looks
  3. Phylogenetic (Cracraft) — smallest monophyletic clade
  4. Ecological (Van Valen) — niche / adaptive zone
⚠ TrapBSC fails on FAH: Fossils, Asexuals, Hybridizers.
Reproductive isolation
⭐"PRE: HTBMG · POST: ISB"Prezygotic: Habitat, Time, Behavior, Mechanical, Gametic. Postzygotic: Inviability, Sterility, Breakdown.
⭐"Heterogametic Hurts First" — Haldane's RuleHybrid sterile/inviable sex = the heterogametic one (XY males in mammals, ZW females in birds).
Speciation modes
⭐"ASP: Allopatric Apart · Sympatric Same · Parapatric Partly"Allopatric = MOST common.
Polyploidy — Auto = self-doubling · Allo = hybrid + doubling. Instant speciation in plants. ~70% of flowering plants.
⭐"Reinforcement: Hybrids Suck → Don't Date Them → Walls Get Thicker"
Real-time speciation
🍎 Rhagoletis apple maggotSympatric speciation, ~140 ya. Apples introduced ~400 ya, hawthorn flies shifted hosts → mating isolation on host plant.
🌼 Tragopogon goatsbeardAllopolyploidy in Pacific NW, ~80 yrs. Observed in real time.

Conservation Genetics

Franklin's 50/500 rule
  • Ne ≥ 50 short-term — avoid inbreeding depression
  • Ne ≥ 500 long-term — maintain adaptive variation
⭐"50 to Survive · 500 to Thrive"
⚠ TrapNe is ALWAYS smaller than N. Effective < census because of unequal sex ratio, reproductive variance, bottlenecks.

Human Evolution
Bipedal first, big brain later · Lucy settles it
Hominin sequence
⭐"Sahel-Ardi-Australo-Habilis-Erectus-Heidi-Neander-Sapiens"Heidelbergensis (~600 KYA) = common ancestor of Sapiens + Neanderthals + Denisovans.
👩‍🦴 LucyAustralopithecus afarensis · 3.2 MYA, Hadar Ethiopia (1974, Johanson) · 40% complete · bipedal pelvis + chimp-sized brain (~400 cc).
⭐"Bipedalism FIRST, Big Brain LATER"— Lucy settles it.
⭐"Forward Foramen = Forward-walking · Rear Foramen = Four-legged"Foramen magnum position = the single anatomical sign of bipedalism in fossils.
Walking trade-offs (4 B's)
⭐"Walking Wrecks: Back, Birth, Bones, Bulges"
  • Back pain (lumbar curve, 80% of adults)
  • Birth difficulty (obstetric dilemma)
  • Bones wear (knee/hip arthritis)
  • Bulges (hernias, gravity on abdomen)
⚠ FOXP2 TrapFOXP2 is NOT "the language gene". It's a transcription factor for orofacial motor control — necessary but not sufficient. Functions in non-human animals (songbirds, mice). Robbins penalizes "language gene."
Brain expansion · 3 hypotheses
⭐"Cooking Calories · Social Skills · Survival Smarts"
  • Cooking (Wrangham) — fire unlocks calories
  • Social Brain (Dunbar) — group size predicts neocortex
  • Ecological — foraging / tool problem-solving
Out-of-Africa: H. erectus leaves first (~1.8 MYA); H. sapiens leaves ~60–70 KYA.
Non-Africans: 1–4% Neanderthal · Melanesians/Papuans: 3–6% Denisovan.
EPAS1 high-altitude variant in Tibetans = inherited from Denisovans.
Pääbo, 2022 Nobel — proved Neanderthal interbreeding via ancient DNA.
